🎬 Want to cast videos from your phone to your TV effortlessly? Learn how to use Google Chromecast and Deep Links without errors in just 3 minutes!

✅ Chromecast - The Smart Way to Cast!
Unlike traditional screen mirroring, Chromecast lets you stream videos directly to your Smart TV with full control over playback, volume, and more. All you need? Your phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Once connected, enjoy seamless casting with zero interruptions!

✅ Deep Linking - A Game-Changer for OTT Apps!
Ever shared a movie link from an OTT app like Netflix or Hotstar? That’s Deep Linking in action! With this feature, users can share direct links to movies and series. When clicked, the link takes them straight to the content inside the app—even if they haven’t installed it yet! If the app isn’t on their device, it prompts them to download it first, skipping unnecessary logins and searches.

💡 An OTT app without deep linking is like a bus without a driver! Don’t miss out on this must-have feature.
